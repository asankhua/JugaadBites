Four menus

A different table for every time of day.

Breakfast is not dinner reheated. Each meal opens its own menu, and only the dishes people really eat at two meals appear in both.

7 to 10 am Breakfast Poha, dosa, idli, upma, parathas 12 to 3 pm Lunch Dal, rice, pulao, sabzi, rajma 4 to 7 pm Snacks Pakora, samosa, chaat, tikki, rolls 7 to 10 pm Dinner Biryani, curries, paneer, noodles

How it works

Three taps from “what now?” to “dinner’s on.”

  1. 01

    Pick your plate

    Choose veg or non-veg, then a meal. Breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner each have their own menu.

  2. 02

    Tick what is here

    Tap one ingredient. The ones that pair with it start to glow, so the next tick is obvious.

  3. 03

    Cook with confidence

    Every recipe shows its ingredients and plain, step-by-step instructions. No guessing, no ads.

Why it helps

Less waste. Fewer takeaway nights. More meals you’re proud of.

No shopping list

Start from what is already in the kitchen, not from a recipe that needs six things you don’t have.

Private by design

No account, no tracking. A refresh clears your choices, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Fast and offline

Every recipe loads with the page. It works on a slow connection, or with none at all.

Start cooking

What can I cook with what is already here?

Choose veg or non-veg and a meal. Each meal opens a different menu. For non-veg, pick egg, meat, or fish first. Then tick an ingredient, and the ones that still match glow.
